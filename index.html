Sidratul Montaha

PhD Student · Computing Science · University of Alberta

I am a PhD student in Computing Science at the University of Alberta. My research lies at the intersection of medical image analysis, deep learning, and computer vision. I'm currently working on RVEF prediction using Cardiac Cine MRI.

Previously, I completed my MSc at the University of Calgary (GPA: 3.85/4), where I worked on privacy-preserving self-supervised learning, novel loss functions for semantic segmentation, and cardiac image analysis. I earned my BSc from Daffodil International University, where my thesis on breast cancer classification led to two Q1 journal publications.

I have published 25+ peer-reviewed papers in venues including IEEE JBHI, IEEE Access, Frontiers in Medicine, PLOS ONE, and Canadian AI.
